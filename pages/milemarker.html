How to Use Milepost Finder
Automatic Route Matching
The finder checks both State / US / Interstate and County Route roadways. It shows a milepost only when one signed route is uniquely confirmed.
Find Nearest Milepost
Tap Find Nearest Milepost and allow location access when prompted. GPS is matched to the authoritative roadway centerline before a milepost is shown.
Review the Result
The result card shows a confirmed milepost, route name, distance from your location, GPS accuracy, and a map pin. If the route cannot be uniquely identified, the page shows no milepost.
Use with Field Notes
Record the milepost in your field notes, work orders, or DC-144 reports. The milepost, route, and distance are shown together for quick reference.
