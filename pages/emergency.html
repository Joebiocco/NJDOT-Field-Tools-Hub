How to Use Emergency Assistance
Call 911 First
For any injury, active hazard, or highway incident, tap Call 911 first.
See Where You Are
The map centers on your current GPS position and marks it with a pulsing You are here marker. The blue circle shows the estimated accuracy area; use Recenter or switch between Street and Satellite as needed.
Share or Copy a Report
Tap Share report to open your phone’s share sheet when available. If sharing is unavailable, the same ready-to-read location summary (coordinates, route, milepost, direction) is copied for dispatch or your field notes.
Tap ? in the header to reopen this guide.

Turn on location access

Location must be turned on and allowed for this page to work. Enable Location Services, then choose Allow for your browser. Without GPS, the map cannot show “You are here” and nearby mileposts cannot be found.

If this is an active emergency, call 911 first. You can return here after location access is enabled.

General Roadside Safety Reminders
  • Turn on hazard lights immediately
  • If safe, move to the shoulder or breakdown lane
  • Stay inside the vehicle if exiting means stepping into traffic
  • Wear a high-visibility vest if available
  • Place cones or flares only if you can do so safely
  • Stay alert for oncoming traffic at all times
General roadside safety practice — not a substitute for official protocol. See the guidelines below for full detail.
