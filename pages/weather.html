How to Use Weather
Choose a Location
Pick an NJ area from the sidebar, grouped by North / Central / South Jersey (pills at the top on mobile), or tap Use My Location for GPS.
Current, Hourly & 7-Day
Current conditions come from the nearest live weather station. The hourly and 7-day panels are the National Weather Service forecast, including chance of precipitation, humidity, and wind. Tap any day in the 7-day list for its full forecast and hourly breakdown.
Active Alerts
Any active NWS alerts for the area appear at the top of the page — tap through for full details on weather.gov.
Jump To & Radar Views
Use the Jump To links to move between Alerts, Current, Radar, Hourly, and 7-Day. Radar defaults to Forecast · HRRR; switch to Observed · NWS for the existing recent radar timeline. Both views include playback, opacity, map style, and NWS alert filters. An internet connection is required; results aren't cached for offline use.
