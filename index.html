Field Tools Hub update

What's new

A quick summary of the major changes across the site.

  • Timesheet Tracker

    A complete payroll workspace with 10-minute entries, lunch and commute deductions, Cash or XP overtime, 35- and 40-hour schedules, holidays, Emergency rates, pay-period history, expanded work logs, and exact Summary Sheet overtime blocks.

  • Bridge Navigator

    Statewide bridge search now uses county data chunks with map selection, GPS lookup, bookmarks, and share or copy tools.

  • Roadway and emergency tools

    Conservative signed-route and milepost matching, clearer roadway results, GPS status and recovery, and safe handling when the evidence is ambiguous.

  • Weather and alerts

    Conditions, forecasts, NWS alerts, radar timeline controls, map alerts, and saved alert settings are brought together in one view.

  • Forms and closeout

    DC-144 templates, signatures, photos, session restore, Excel export, and improved Work Order closeout PDF and photo handling.

  • Hub and offline improvements

    Refreshed responsive layouts, dark mode, safer transitions, install and bookmark help, local persistence, and a safer offline install — the app now fails to update rather than activate with a partially-cached core.
